How a project runs

  1. Scoping call. We agree who uses the report and the decisions it supports.
  2. Written requirements. Data sources, keys, KPI definitions and existing Excel reports are documented.
  3. Timeline agreed. Milestones and deadlines are confirmed before build starts.
  4. Model and build. Data is cleaned and modelled, then the report is built on a validated dataset.
  5. Review with you. You test the numbers against your own and we refine together.
  6. Handover. Documentation of measures and data flow, plus a team walkthrough.
